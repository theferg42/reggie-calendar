Vault Calendar privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What it accesses

Vault Calendar uses two Google permissions. calendar.app.created lets it create and update events only on the calendar it created itself. gmail.send lets it send email; it cannot read, list, search or delete any email. It cannot read your other calendars, your mailbox, your contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

The app writes events the owner has approved, such as a title, date, time and a short note, to that one calendar, and sends emails the owner has approved, each with a one-time code, to the recipients named in them. It sends at most a few emails a day. It does not use the data for anything else.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's account. Event details and sent emails are kept there as a local record of what was written and sent. Nothing is stored on any other server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
