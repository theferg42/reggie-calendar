Vault Calendar privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What it accesses

Vault Calendar uses one Google permission: calendar.app.created. With it, the app can only create and update events on the calendar it created itself. It cannot read your other calendars, your email, your contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

The app writes events the owner has approved, such as a title, date, time and a short note, to that one calendar. It does not use the data for anything else.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's account. Event details are kept there as a local record of what was written. Nothing is stored on any other server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
