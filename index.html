Vault Calendar

Vault Calendar is a private, single-user tool. It adds and updates events on one Google Calendar that it created itself, called "Reggie", after the owner approves each event.

It also sends a small number of plain emails the owner has approved, such as quote requests to businesses, from the assistant's own Gmail address.

It asks Google for two narrow permissions: to manage calendars and events that this app created (calendar.app.created), and to send email (gmail.send). It cannot see or change any other calendar, and it cannot read, search or delete any email.

It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for.
